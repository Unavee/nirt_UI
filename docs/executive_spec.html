Solar CRM & Project Management System

An industrial operating platform engineered for end-to-end Solar EPC governance across Residential (PM Surya Ghar), Commercial & Industrial (C&I), and Agricultural solar installations: from omnichannel lead intake and 80% upfront advance collection to automated customer tracking, DISCOM net-metering approvals, and PM Surya Ghar subsidy disbursement.

1. Executive Summary & System Philosophy

Solar photovoltaic engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) is an operationally intensive business. A single commercial or residential solar installation involves up to 17 sequential milestones, intersects 8 specialized departments, requires liaison with external government entities (TANGEDCO and the PM Surya Ghar National Portal), and demands stringent documentation across physical site surveys, electrical safety audits, civil structure installations, and banking/DBT subsidy workflows.

Generic sales CRMs fail because they treat solar as a simple sales transaction. In solar EPC, the sale is merely the starting line. The real risks of margin erosion, customer dissatisfaction, and regulatory stalls occur post-sale: during DISCOM net-metering approvals, material staging, physical installation, and subsidy release.

The 5 Immutable System Rules:
  1. One Customer – One Master Record: A unified identity eliminating duplicated customer profiles across marketing, sales, and accounts.
  2. One Project – One Digital Folder: Every contract, survey photo, SLD drawing, bill, and test certificate is bound permanently to a unified Project ID (NIRT-YYMM-XXXX).
  3. Clear Ownership & Deadline Governance: Every single task across all 17 stages has a named responsible owner, department, planned SLA turnaround target, and escalation trigger.
  4. Hard Stage Gates: Commercial and procurement actions cannot proceed without prerequisite sign-offs (e.g., procurement is hard-locked until Accounts logs the Stage 7 Advance receipt).
  5. The 3 Mandatory Closures: A project cannot be marked completed until it achieves Technical Closure (commissioning report + net meter sync), Commercial Closure (balance = ₹0), and Subsidy Closure (DBT bank credit confirmed via UTR).

1.1 Strategic Ground Realities & Operational Objectives

Engineered specifically for multi-sector solar installations across Tamil Nadu (covering Residential, C&I, and Agricultural sectors), the system enforces three critical operational disciplines:

  • Commercial Working Capital Protection: Procurement is strictly hard-locked until an 80% upfront advance is cleared in Stage 7, eliminating working capital dead-lock on supplier panels and inverters.
  • Standardized Field Survey Methodology: Domestic terraces, commercial sheds, and agricultural sites do not utilize complex CAD models; field technicians capture physical tape dimensions, shadow clearances, 4 mandatory photo slots (solar installation area, meter board, LT pole, stairs), and attach standard TANGEDCO Single Line Diagrams (SLD).
  • Automated Regulatory Alignment: Prevents PM Surya Ghar subsidy claim rejections by cross-validating consumer EB numbers, Aadhaar identities, and bank passbooks prior to portal filing.

TECH STACK & BACKEND

9. Mobile Field Forms & On-Site Data Entry Standards

To ensure straightforward, reliable use by ground technicians on customer premises and installation sites without requiring complex technical training, all field workflows use standard, structured mobile input forms:

  • Simple Step-by-Step Form Layout: Clear, vertical single-column mobile forms with large text boxes, simple number inputs, and explicit Save / Submit buttons, eliminating confusing nested menus.
  • High-Contrast Outdoor Readability: High-contrast black labels on white input boxes and clear form borders, ensuring field staff can read and complete forms under outdoor terrace lighting.
  • Offline Form Saving (Zero Signal Safe): Field personnel can fill and save survey forms even in remote field areas, terraces, or basements with zero mobile network; data is stored securely on the device and uploaded once connectivity returns.
  • Standard 4-Slot Photo Upload Fields: Straightforward file/camera upload fields directly inside the survey form to attach the 4 required verification photos (1. Proposed Solar Area View, 2. EB Meter Board, 3. Main LT Panel, 4. Inverter Wall) before submission.
  • Structured Manual Technical Input Fields: Plain, standardized input boxes for entering site measurements manually—tape length (ft), tape width (ft), usable shadow-free area (sq.ft), sanctioned load (kW), electricity phase selection (1-Phase / 3-Phase), and roof type (RCC Slab / Tile / Metal Sheet).
  • On-Site Digital Signature Capture: A basic digital signature box directly on the mobile form where the customer signs off on survey agreement, material delivery receipts (GRN), and installation completion.

10. Backend Data Architecture, RLS Security & Audit Engine

Grounded in the technical clarifications, the backend is architected for enterprise data integrity, regulatory compliance, and multi-team security:

  • PostgreSQL Relational Database & Row-Level Security (RLS): Hardened database security where access policies are enforced at the database level. Sales executives cannot view private banking balances; field technicians cannot modify pricing; accounts alone can release advance gates.
  • Automated Field-Level Audit Trail (BFRD Sec 34 Compliance): Every record mutation triggers an immutable audit log capturing user_id, timestamp, table_name, column_name, old_value, and new_value for complete management accountability.
  • Live Deduplication Engine: Database unique indexing automatically validates 10-digit primary mobile numbers and TANGEDCO EB Consumer Numbers across all 13 lead channels in real time.
  • Encrypted Digital Document Locker (BFRD Sec 30): Secure cloud object storage with signed URL access tokens for KYC documents, Aadhaar cards, property tax receipts, TANGEDCO sanction letters, and ICR completion certificates.
  • Event-Driven Webhook Dispatcher: Asynchronous message broker delivering automated bilingual WhatsApp Business API templates and transactional emails without blocking UI performance.

11. Core Application Modules & Functional Capabilities

11.1 Module 1: Lead Capture, Deduplication & Disqualification Engine

  • Omnichannel Sourcing: Captures leads from 13 sources (Direct, Referrals, Digital, WhatsApp, Architects, Builders, Contractors, etc.).
  • Real-time Deduplication: Prevents duplicate entries on 10-digit Mobile and TANGEDCO EB Consumer Numbers.
  • Unqualified Lead & Lost-Reason Audit (BFRD Sec 6): Mandatory root-cause logging when a lead is rejected or lost (e.g., Weak/Asbestos Roof, Low Sanctioned Load, Tenant Without Ownership, Price / Competitor Selected, or Postponed) to preserve marketing intelligence.
  • Instant Solar Sizer: Computes capacity (kW), capital cost, PM Surya Ghar subsidy (₹30k/1kW, ₹60k/2kW, ₹78k/3kW+), and annual savings.

11.2 Module 2: Technical Site Survey & Feasibility Audit

  • Structural Assessment: Records tape measurements (length × width), shadow-free area (sq.ft), roof type, and orientation.
  • Electrical Infrastructure Inspection: Evaluates sanctioned load, phase (Single vs 3-Phase), LT panel location, and chemical earthing space.
  • Safety Standard: Enforces earthing pit location audit to guarantee soil resistance < 5 Ω with dedicated Lightning Arrestor (LA) clearance.
  • Mandatory 4-Slot Photo Verification: Upload slots for (1) Proposed Solar Installation Area, (2) EB meter, (3) Main DB panel, and (4) Proposed inverter wall.

11.3 Module 3: Quad-Pipeline Control Board & SLA Delay Tracking

  1. Sales Pipeline: New Lead → Contacted → Qualified → Site Survey → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Deal Won.
  2. Project Execution Pipeline: BOM Unlocked → DISCOM Submitted → Procurement PO → Site Installation → Pre-Commissioning → CEIG.
  3. Financial & Settlement Pipeline: 80% Upfront Advance → 80% Advance Cleared → 10% Installation Balance → 10% Final Settlement (Zero Due).
  4. PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Pipeline: Customer Verified → Portal Claim Lodged → DISCOM Verification → Sanctioned → DBT Disbursed.
  • Process Deadline & Delay Monitoring Engine (BFRD Sec 28): Compares Planned Date vs. Actual Date across every stage. Automatically highlights overdue bottlenecks (e.g., +3 Days Overdue) on the PMO dashboard with manager escalation triggers.

11.4 Module 4: Proposal Sizer & Official Quotation Engine

Standard Commercial Payment Terms (NIRT Operational Policy):
  • 80% — Initial Advance Payment upon Site Visit & Order Confirmation: NIRT's standard operational model requires an 80% upfront commitment upon successful site survey and feasibility sign-off. Secures immediate factory BOM procurement without working capital lockup.
  • 10% — Site Installation & Mechanical/Electrical Testing Completion: Released upon mechanical mounting of modules, inverter placement, DC/AC cabling, and earthing pit resistance sign-off (< 5 Ω).
  • 10% — Net Metering Synchronization & Handover: Final settlement upon TANGEDCO bi-directional meter installation and grid synchronization.

11.5 Module 5: 360° Project Dossier

  • TANGEDCO Net Metering Tracker: 8-step matrix (Application → Feasibility → Readiness → Inspection → Audit → Meter Sanction → Meter Install → Grid Sync).
  • PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Ledger: National Portal application tracking, claim reference numbers, and bank transaction UTR logger.
  • Milestone Payment Ledger: Live balance tracking showing total project value, 80% initial advance received, outstanding balance, and final settlement status.
  • Document Locker: Categorized repository for KYC documents, Aadhaar cards, EB bills, signed contracts, SLD diagrams, and Installation Reports (ICRs).
